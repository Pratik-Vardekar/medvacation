Frequently Asked Questions

  • How to select a medical tourism destination?

    Destinations specialize in specific treatments; for example, some are renowned for cancer treatment, while others excel in heart surgery. Read our blog on "famous destinations for various treatments" for more info.

  • How to plan medical tourism travel?

    Planning medical travel abroad can be overwhelming. At Plan Medical Tourism, we provide a step-by-step guide and attend to all your needs for a seamless experience.

  • For which treatments is India more preferred?

    India is preferred for joint replacements, cancer surgeries, organ transplants, heart surgeries, dental treatments, and more. Check our blog for detailed information on popular treatments.

  • Which cities have direct flights to India?

    Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore are well-connected cities with direct international flights from many countries.

  • Who travels to India for medical tourism?

    Medical tourists from countries like Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, Maldives, Indonesia, and Kenya travel to India for treatments, along with Non-Resident Indians worldwide.

  • How do I select a doctor in India?

    Choose a doctor based on credentials, experience, patient reviews, awards, and memberships in medical associations. Check online reviews and other resources to make an informed choice.
